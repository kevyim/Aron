# Post-Production Specification

## Video
- Resolution: 1080x1920 (vertical/Reels/TikTok) or 3840x2160 (horizontal/YouTube)
- Frame Rate: 24fps (cinematic)
- Codec: H.264 High Profile
- Bitrate: 20 Mbps minimum
- Duration: 40.0 seconds

## Color Grade
- Lift: Warm amber shift (+15 warmth)
- Gamma: Desaturated beige midtones
- Gain: Clean highlights, no clipping
- Overall: Luxury café aesthetic — warm but not orange

## Typography
- Primary Font: SF Pro Display Light (or Helvetica Neue Ultralight)
- Weight: Ultralight / Thin
- Color: White (#FFFFFF) at 90% opacity
- Tracking: +50 (wide letter spacing)
- All caps for taglines

## Audio
- Music: Lo-fi ambient pad, warm analog feel
- SFX: Liquid pour (Scene 2), ice clink (Scene 2), pearl bounce (Scene 3)
- All SFX: Foley-quality, not stock
- Music duck to -12dB under SFX hits
- Final 3 seconds: Music fade out

## Transitions
- Between clips: Cross-dissolve, 0.5 seconds
- Text overlays: Fade in 0.5s, hold 2s, fade out 0.3s
- No fast cuts, no wipes, no zoom transitions

## Pacing
- Slow, controlled, confident
- Every frame should feel intentional
- Think: Apple product video meets Starbucks beverage ad

## Export Presets

### Instagram Reels / TikTok
- 1080x1920, 24fps, H.264, 15 Mbps

### YouTube
- 3840x2160, 24fps, H.264, 50 Mbps

### Website Hero
- 1920x1080, 24fps, H.264, 20 Mbps, loop-friendly
